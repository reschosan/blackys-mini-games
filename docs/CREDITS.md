# Third-party credits

This page lists third-party assets used by the game and the licenses that apply to them. The project-owned code and content have a separate [license](../LICENSE.md). The in-game Credits screen is a shorter attribution list.

## Toon Cat FREE

- **Creator:** [Omabuarts Studio](https://sketchfab.com/omabuarts)
- **Source:** [Toon Cat FREE on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/toon-cat-free-b2bd1ee7858444bda366110a2d960386)
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **File:** `public/models/cat.glb`
- **Changes:** The baked gradient texture is darkened and multiplied by the selected fur color in `src/shared/cat/catPatternMaterial.ts`. The model is scaled for the kart and rotated 90° to align its forward direction with the game. Its walking animation is played according to kart speed. Additional procedural motions for leaning, tail, ears, boost, and idle use the existing bones; they are not part of the original model.

## Quaternius model packs

The following packs are by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), distributed under CC0. Attribution is provided voluntarily. The project owner confirmed the licenses for the Nature and Space packs on 4 October 2026 and the other three packs on 9 October 2026. Only the models used by the game are included.

| Pack | Used for | Files in this repository | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stylized Nature MegaKit | Trees, rocks, grass, bushes, and flowers in the nature map and arenas | `public/models/nature-pack/` | Models are scaled and instanced; grass also covers the driving surface |
| Ultimate Space Kit | Space map, arena scenery, and the four jet types | `public/models/space-pack/` | Models are scaled, instanced, or cloned; included animations play on characters |
| Farm Buildings Bundle and Animated Animal Pack | Farm buildings, cows, and horses | `public/models/farm-pack/` | Models are scaled and instanced; included animal animations play |
| Ultimate Fantasy RTS | Medieval town buildings, castle, market, towers, and harbor | `public/models/town-pack/` | Models are scaled and instanced |
| Cyberpunk Game Kit | Streetlights, signs, antennae, air conditioners, and platforms in the night city | `public/models/city-pack/` | Models are scaled and instanced |

The four fighter-jet models come from Ultimate Space Kit: `Spaceship.glb`, `Spaceship-Jqfed124pQ.glb`, `Spaceship-u105mYHLHU.glb`, and `Spaceship-VSxUAFhzbA.glb`. Their game assignments are in `src/shared/config/planeTypes.ts`. Arena scenery reuses models from the Nature, Farm, and Fantasy packs.

## Libraries used for phone invitations

These are code dependencies listed in `package.json`, not game artwork.

- `qrcode` 1.5.4 — MIT, Ryan Day and contributors; generates QR codes. `@types/qrcode` provides development-only types under MIT.
- `jsQR` 1.4.0 — Apache 2.0, Cosmo Wolfe; reads QR codes from the camera, loaded when the camera opens.
- Caddy — Apache 2.0; used only as the `caddy:2-alpine` Docker image for local HTTPS preview, not included in the game build.

The game's sounds and music are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API in `src/shared/audio/`. They do not use external recordings or samples. Karts, most environment surfaces, and arena textures are generated by project code.
